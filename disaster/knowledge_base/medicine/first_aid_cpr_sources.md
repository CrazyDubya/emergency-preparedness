# First Aid and CPR Training Sources

This guide lists reputable sources for first aid, CPR, and emergency medical training.

## Certification Courses

*   **American Red Cross** – [redcross.org/take-a-class](https://www.redcross.org/take-a-class)  
    First Aid, CPR/AED, babysitting, lifeguarding. Online and in-person options.

*   **American Heart Association** – [heart.org/cpr](https://cpr.heart.org)  
    BLS, Heartsaver CPR/AED, First Aid. Widely accepted for workplace and healthcare.

*   **National Safety Council** – [nsc.org/firstaid](https://www.nsc.org/firstaid)  
    First Aid, CPR, workplace safety.

*   **Stop the Bleed** – [stopthebleed.org](https://www.stopthebleed.org)  
    Free training for controlling severe bleeding. Often offered by hospitals and emergency services.

## Free Online Resources

*   **CDC First Aid** – [cdc.gov/firstaid](https://www.cdc.gov/firstaid)  
    Basic first aid guidance.

*   **Ready.gov First Aid** – [ready.gov/first-aid-kit](https://www.ready.gov/first-aid-kit)  
    Kit contents and basic use.

*   **NIH MedlinePlus First Aid** – [medlineplus.gov/firstaid](https://medlineplus.gov/firstaid.html)  
    Condition-specific first aid information.

## What to Look For in a Course

*   Hands-on practice with manikins
*   Certification valid for 2 years (typical for CPR/AED)
*   Coverage of choking, bleeding, shock, and cardiac arrest
*   Pediatric module if you care for children
