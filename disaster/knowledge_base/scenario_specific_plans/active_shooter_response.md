# Active Shooter Response Guide

This guide summarizes the **Run. Hide. Fight.** approach to surviving an active shooter incident. Based on [Ready.gov Active Shooter](https://www.ready.gov/active-shooter) and [DHS guidance](https://www.dhs.gov/active-shooter).

## Run

*   **Getting away is the top priority.** Leave belongings behind. Evacuate if safe to do so.
*   **Warn others** nearby if you can do so safely.
*   **Call 911** once you reach safety.
*   **Know exits:** Before emergencies, identify two nearby exits when visiting buildings. Plan escape routes.

## Hide

*   **If you cannot escape safely,** find a secure hiding place out of the shooter's view.
*   **Lock and barricade** doors. Turn off lights. Silence electronic devices. Stay quiet.
*   **Spread out** — do not hide in groups; make it harder for the shooter to target multiple people.
*   **Communicate with police** silently (texts, window signs) if possible.
*   **Stay in place** until law enforcement confirms the danger has passed.

## Fight

*   **Last resort** — when in immediate danger and you cannot run or hide.
*   **Commit to your actions.** Act aggressively to stop the shooter.
*   **Use improvised weapons:** chairs, fire extinguishers, scissors, books — to distract and disarm.

## Before an Incident

*   Familiarize yourself with emergency plans at locations you frequent.
*   Participate in active shooter drills.
*   Learn first aid and tourniquet training.
*   Report suspicious activity to authorities.

## After an Incident

*   **Keep hands visible and empty** when encountering law enforcement.
*   **Follow their instructions** immediately.
*   **Seek professional help** to cope with trauma.
