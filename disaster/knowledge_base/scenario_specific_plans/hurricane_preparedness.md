# Hurricane Preparedness Guide

This guide provides actionable steps for what to do before, during, and after a hurricane.

## Before Hurricane Season: Preparation

*   [ ] **Know Your Zone:** Determine if you are in an evacuation zone. Sign up for local alerts.
*   [ ] **Build a Kit:** Water (1 gallon per person per day, 7+ days), non-perishable food, first aid, medications, flashlight, batteries, cash, important documents.
*   [ ] **Strengthen Your Home:** Install storm shutters or board-up capability. Secure loose outdoor items. Trim trees.
*   [ ] **Plan Evacuation:** Know your evacuation routes. Have a destination and fuel your vehicle ahead of time.
*   [ ] **Review Insurance:** Ensure adequate coverage. Flood insurance has a 30-day wait period.
*   [ ] **See also:** [Ready.gov Hurricanes](https://www.ready.gov/hurricanes).

## During a Hurricane: Stay Safe

*   **If evacuation is ordered:**
    *   Leave immediately. Follow official evacuation routes.
    *   Take your emergency kit and pets.
*   **If sheltering in place:**
    *   Stay indoors, away from windows and glass doors.
    *   Go to an interior room, closet, or bathroom on the lowest level.
    *   Avoid elevators. Use stairs.
    *   Listen to NOAA Weather Radio or local news for updates.
*   **During the eye:** The calm center is temporary. Dangerous winds will return from the opposite direction.

## After the Hurricane: Recovery

*   [ ] **Wait for the all-clear** before going outside.
*   [ ] **Avoid floodwaters and downed power lines.**
*   [ ] **Check on neighbors,** especially elderly or those with special needs.
*   [ ] **Document damage** for insurance. Take photos before cleanup.
*   [ ] **Use generators outdoors only** to prevent carbon monoxide poisoning.
*   [ ] **See also:** [FEMA Hurricane](https://www.fema.gov/disaster/hurricane), community recovery resources.
