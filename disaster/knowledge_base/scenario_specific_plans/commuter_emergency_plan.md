# Commuter Emergency Plan Guide

Emergencies can occur without warning, and you may not be at home. This guide helps you plan for disasters while traveling between work, home, and other regularly visited locations.

## Key Components

Document the following and keep a copy in your wallet or an accessible location:

*   **Normal and alternative routes** to home, work, school, and other frequent destinations.
*   **Public transportation options:** Bus, train, ferry — line numbers, stops, fares.
*   **Daily driving directions** and alternative routes to and from work.
*   **Transportation contacts:** Taxi companies, commuter rail, bus lines.
*   **Local traffic and transportation information** (radio stations, apps, websites).

## Supplies for Commuters

*   **Go-bag or car kit:** Water, snacks, first aid, flashlight, phone charger, cash.
*   **Comfortable shoes** for walking if transit or roads are disrupted.
*   **Emergency contact list** (on paper, not only on your phone).

## Planning Tips

*   **Identify shelter locations** along your routes (schools, libraries, hospitals, stores).
*   **Coordinate with family** on meeting points if you cannot get home.
*   **Know your employer's emergency plan** — evacuation, lockdown, communication.
*   **Update your plan** when routes or schedules change.
