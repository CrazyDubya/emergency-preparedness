# Extreme Heat Preparedness Guide

This guide provides actionable steps for what to do before, during, and after periods of extreme heat.

## Before Extreme Heat: Preparation

*   [ ] **Stay Informed:** Know the heat index and heat advisory levels in your area.
*   [ ] **Cooling Plan:** Identify air-conditioned locations (libraries, malls, cooling centers) if you don't have AC.
*   [ ] **Hydration:** Have plenty of water. Avoid alcohol and caffeine during heat events.
*   [ ] **Vulnerable People:** Check on older adults, young children, and those with chronic conditions. Never leave anyone in a parked car.
*   [ ] **Home Prep:** Install window reflectors, use fans, ensure AC units are serviced.
*   [ ] **See also:** [Ready.gov Heat](https://www.ready.gov/heat), [CDC Extreme Heat](https://www.cdc.gov/disasters/extremeheat).

## During Extreme Heat: Stay Cool and Hydrated

*   **Indoors:**
    *   Stay in air conditioning when possible. Use fans to improve ventilation.
    *   Take cool showers or baths.
    *   Avoid using the stove or oven.
*   **Outdoors:**
    *   Limit outdoor activity to early morning or evening.
    *   Wear lightweight, light-colored, loose-fitting clothing.
    *   Wear sunscreen and a hat.
    *   Drink water frequently. Don't wait until you're thirsty.
*   **Know the Signs of Heat Illness:**
    *   Heat cramps, heat exhaustion (heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, nausea), heat stroke (high body temp, confusion, loss of consciousness). Heat stroke is a medical emergency—call 911.

## After a Heat Event: Recovery

*   [ ] **Continue to hydrate** and rest.
*   [ ] **Check on vulnerable neighbors.**
*   [ ] **Avoid sudden return to strenuous activity.**
