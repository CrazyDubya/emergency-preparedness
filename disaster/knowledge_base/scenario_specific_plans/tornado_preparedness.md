# Tornado Preparedness Guide

This guide provides actionable steps for what to do before, during, and after a tornado.

## Before a Tornado: Preparation

*   [ ] **Identify a Safe Room:** A basement, storm cellar, or an interior room on the lowest floor with no windows (bathroom, closet, hallway).
*   [ ] **Practice Drills:** Know how to get to your safe room quickly. Include pets in your plan.
*   [ ] **Emergency Kit:** Water, non-perishable food, first aid kit, flashlight, batteries, whistle, important documents.
*   [ ] **Sign Up for Alerts:** Wireless Emergency Alerts (WEA), NOAA Weather Radio, and local emergency alerts.
*   [ ] **Know the Signs:** Dark or greenish sky, large hail, roaring noise (like a freight train), rotating funnel cloud.
*   [ ] **See also:** [Ready.gov Tornadoes](https://www.ready.gov/tornadoes).

## During a Tornado: Take Cover Immediately

*   **If you are in a structure (home, school, office):**
    *   Go to the lowest level. Get under a sturdy table or desk.
    *   Cover your head and neck with your arms. Use pillows, blankets, or a mattress if available.
    *   Stay away from windows and exterior walls.
*   **If you are in a mobile home:**
    *   Get out. Go to the nearest sturdy building or designated storm shelter.
*   **If you are outdoors with no shelter:**
    *   Lie flat in a ditch or low-lying area. Cover your head.
    *   Do not get under an overpass or bridge.
*   **If you are in a vehicle:**
    *   Drive to the nearest sturdy shelter. If you cannot, stay in the car with seatbelt on, head below windows, or get out and lie in a low area.

## After a Tornado: Stay Safe

*   [ ] **Watch for hazards:** Downed power lines, broken gas lines, sharp debris.
*   [ ] **Check on others:** Help neighbors and those who may be injured.
*   [ ] **Listen to officials:** Stay out of damaged areas until cleared.
*   [ ] **Document damage** for insurance claims.
*   [ ] **See also:** [FEMA Tornado](https://www.fema.gov/disaster/tornado).
