# Winter Storm Preparedness Guide

## Before the Storm

*   **Prepare your home:** Insulate walls and attics, caulk and weatherstrip doors and windows. Clear gutters and downspouts.
*   **Heating fuel:** Ensure you have adequate heating fuel. If you use wood, have a good supply of dry wood.
*   **Emergency kit:** Assemble a kit with non-perishable food, water, blankets, flashlights, batteries, a first-aid kit, and a battery-powered or hand-crank radio.
*   **Vehicle:** Keep your vehicle's gas tank full. Check antifreeze, tires, and windshield wiper fluid.
*   **Communication plan:** Establish a family communication plan in case you are separated.

## During the Storm

*   **Stay indoors:** Avoid unnecessary travel. If you must go out, dress in layers and wear waterproof, insulated boots.
*   **Conserve heat:** Close off unused rooms. Stuff towels or rags in cracks under doors. Cover windows at night.
*   **Food and water:** Eat regularly to maintain body heat. Drink plenty of fluids to prevent dehydration.
*   **Carbon monoxide:** Use generators, charcoal grills, and camping stoves outdoors only. Keep them away from windows.
*   **Frozen pipes:** To prevent pipes from freezing, keep faucets dripping slightly. Open cabinet doors to allow warmer air to circulate around plumbing.

## After the Storm

*   **Check on neighbors:** Especially the elderly or those with disabilities.
*   **Power outages:** Report outages to your utility company. Avoid downed power lines.
*   **Travel:** Be aware of icy roads and sidewalks. Clear snow and ice from walkways.
*   **Food safety:** Discard perishable food that has been at room temperature for more than two hours.
