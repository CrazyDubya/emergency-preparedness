# Pandemic Preparedness Guide

This guide provides actionable steps for what to do before, during, and after a pandemic or widespread infectious disease outbreak.

## Before a Pandemic: Preparation

*   [ ] **Build Supplies:** Two-week supply of food, water, medications, and household essentials. Include supplies for pets.
*   [ ] **Medical Supplies:** First aid kit, fever reducers, tissues, soap, hand sanitizer, face masks (if recommended).
*   [ ] **Plan for Disruption:** Schools, workplaces, and services may close. Have backup childcare and work-from-home arrangements.
*   [ ] **Stay Informed:** Know trusted sources (CDC, WHO, local health department). Sign up for health alerts.
*   [ ] **See also:** [Ready.gov Pandemic](https://www.ready.gov/pandemic), [CDC Pandemic Preparedness](https://www.cdc.gov/flu/pandemic-resources).

## During a Pandemic: Protect Yourself and Others

*   **Hygiene:**
    *   Wash hands frequently with soap and water (20 seconds).
    *   Avoid touching face, eyes, nose, mouth.
    *   Cover coughs and sneezes with elbow or tissue.
*   **Distance:**
    *   Follow local guidance on social distancing and gatherings.
    *   Stay home when sick.
*   **Masks:** Use face masks when recommended by health authorities.
*   **Vaccination:** Get recommended vaccines when available.
*   **Mental Health:** Stay connected with family and friends (virtually). Limit news consumption if it causes stress.

## After a Pandemic Wave: Recovery and Preparedness

*   [ ] **Restock supplies** for the next wave or future event.
*   [ ] **Support community recovery** and those who were affected.
*   [ ] **Update your plan** based on lessons learned.
