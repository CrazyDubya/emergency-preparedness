# Landslide Preparedness Guide

This guide provides actionable steps for what to do before, during, and after a landslide.

## Before a Landslide: Preparation

*   [ ] **Know Your Risk:** Steep slopes, areas that have slid before, and regions with heavy rain or wildfires are at higher risk.
*   [ ] **Consult Experts:** Have a geological assessment if you live on or below steep slopes.
*   [ ] **Emergency Plan:** Identify evacuation routes. Have a family meeting place.
*   [ ] **Emergency Kit:** Water, food, first aid, flashlight, batteries, important documents.
*   [ ] **Property Measures:** Install flexible pipe fittings, consider retaining walls or drainage improvements. Avoid building on steep slopes.
*   [ ] **See also:** [Ready.gov Landslides](https://www.ready.gov/landslides-debris-flow), [USGS Landslides](https://www.usgs.gov/natural-hazards/landslide-hazards).

## During a Landslide: Evacuate if Possible

*   **If you suspect imminent danger:**
    *   Evacuate immediately. Inform neighbors if safe to do so.
*   **If you cannot evacuate:**
    *   Curl into a tight ball and protect your head.
    *   Move to the side of the flow, not in the path.
*   **Watch for warning signs:**
    *   Cracks in the ground, tilting trees or fences, unusual sounds (rumbling, trees cracking).
    *   Sudden changes in water flow (muddy water, new springs).

## After a Landslide: Stay Safe

*   [ ] **Stay away from the slide area**—additional slides may occur.
*   [ ] **Check for injured or trapped people** without entering the slide area. Call 911.
*   [ ] **Listen for unusual sounds** (trees cracking, boulders rolling) that may indicate more movement.
*   [ ] **Report broken utilities** to the appropriate authorities.
*   [ ] **See also:** [FEMA Landslide](https://www.fema.gov/disaster/landslide), community recovery resources.
