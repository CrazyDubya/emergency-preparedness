SURVIVE CORE. Preparedness aid, not a pro. If a phone/radio works, call your emergency number; obey orders.
ORDER (rule of 3): air ~3 min, heavy bleeding/cold ~minutes, water ~3 days, food ~3 weeks. Fix the fastest killer first. Keep group warm, dry, hydrated.
NEVER run engines, generators, grills, or stoves indoors: carbon monoxide kills unseen. Ventilate. Leave NOW for gas smell, smoke, fire, leaning walls, or rising water.
BLEEDING: press hard 10+ min; limb gushing? strap a hand-width above, tighten till it stops, note time.
NOT BREATHING: push hard and fast, chest center, ~2/sec. Choking: back blows, belly thrusts.
WATER: settle and strain if cloudy, THEN boil 1 min (3 up high) OR add 2 drops plain bleach per liter, wait 30 min. Neither removes chemicals.
WARMTH: stay dry, layer, insulate off ground. Too hot: shade, wet skin, rest.
CLEAN: wash hands; keep waste far from water and food.
SIGNAL: three of anything (whistle/fire/flash) = HELP.
STAY if safe/known; GO if threatened - leave a note: where/when.
