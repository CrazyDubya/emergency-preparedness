EMERGENCY CORE
1 Danger: fire/smoke/gas/unstable structure/flood/violence? Leave. Never run generators, grills, charcoal or fuel heaters indoors; CO kills unseen.
2 Life: severe bleeding—direct pressure; pack deep wounds if able. Not breathing—get help; CPR/AED if trained. Unconscious but breathing: side position. Avoid moving spine injury unless danger requires it.
3 Water: use sealed water first. If unsafe, clear water: boil 1 min (3 min above 6500 ft/2000 m), cool covered. Otherwise use proven filter/disinfectant as labeled. Keep feces away from water.
4 Shelter: stay dry, block wind, insulate from ground. Heat/cold can kill before hunger.
5 Sanitation: wash hands after waste/before food; isolate feces from people, food, water.
6 Food: discard suspect perishables; don't taste-test.
7 Comms: conserve battery. Set meeting place/check-in times. SMS/radio may work when calls fail.
8 Stop; assess people, water, shelter, medical/fire; inventory supplies; act on greatest threat. Don't create a second victim.
