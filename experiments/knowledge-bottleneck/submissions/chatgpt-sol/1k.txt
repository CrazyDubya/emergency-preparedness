SURVIVAL CORE
DANGER: Leave fire, gas/fumes, rising flood, unstable buildings, downed wires. Call help if available.
BLEEDING: Firm pressure. Pack deep wound with gauze/cloth. If life-threatening limb bleeding continues, tourniquet above wound/not joint; note time; don't loosen.
BREATHING: Unresponsive, not breathing normally: get help/AED; hard-fast chest compressions, center chest, 100-120/min.
WATER: Prefer known-safe. Cloudy: cloth-filter, then rolling boil 1 min (3 min high altitude). Boiling cannot remove fuel, salt, metals/chemicals: find another source.
DIARRHEA: frequent safe fluids. ORS: 1 L safe water + 6 level tsp sugar + 1/2 tsp salt.
AIR: Never run generators, grills, charcoal or engines indoors/garage; CO kills silently.
HYGIENE: Keep feces from food/water; wash hands after toilet/before food.
TEMP: Stay dry; insulate from ground/wind/cold. Heat: shade, rest, fluids, wet skin.
FOOD: If doubtful, discard.
PEOPLE: Work together; inventory water/food/meds/tools; conserve battery; set meeting place
